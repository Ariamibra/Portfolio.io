01 / ACADEMIC RESEARCHUniversity Team Project · Capstone
Industrial GIS · 2000–2025

Dammam Industrial Cities
Spatial & Temporal Analysis

A 25-year spatial and temporal analysis of Dammam’s three industrial cities (D1, D2, D3), built on a geodatabase of 120 factory records across nine industry sectors, each cross-checked against 200+ official and public sources for verification.

THE QUESTION

How has industrial development evolved spatially and temporally across Dammam’s three industrial cities between 2000 and 2025?

MY ROLE

Where the academic research mapped and analyzed industrial spatial and temporal change, this independent extension goes further: it measures comparative industrial development readiness by converting the GIS analysis outputs into five standardized indicators.

THE QUESTION

Does current industrial strength tell the whole story of future development readiness?

Key insight Current industrial strength ≠ future development readiness.

Built on the five equally-weighted IDRM indicators — Industrial Density (IDI), Logistics Accessibility (LAI), Expansion Potential (IEPI), Industrial Growth (IGI), and Industrial Specialization (ISI) — and presented through one interactive Power BI dashboard with four analytical views: Executive Overview, Spatial & Accessibility, Growth & Industrial Structure, and City Profiles & IDRM. The model is a comparative screening framework, not a predictive forecast. Data roles differ: city-level inputs (official factory counts, areas, land use) come from MODON, while growth and specialization use an author-compiled, manually verified sample of 40 factories per city (120 in total) — not the official MODON factory total. Spatial analysis was performed in ArcGIS Pro.
